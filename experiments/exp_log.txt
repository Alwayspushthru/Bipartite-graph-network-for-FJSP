20260530_pair_norm setting
Baseline: recurrent PPO + delay gating, actor+critic both use history, hist_dim=32. fea_j and fea_m are per-env standardized; fea_pairs was NOT normalized.
Change: add per-env masked standardization to construct_pair_features() at end of each step, normalizing over feasible (job, machine) pairs only — consistent with fea_j/fea_m treatment.
Hypothesis: raw time-valued pair features (start, wait_job, wait_mach) accumulate and grow with episode length; a 40x10 instance has ~2x the episode length of 20x10 training, causing systematic scale mismatch. Normalizing should remove this bias and improve SD1/SD2 generalization to larger sizes, and also help BenchData OOD.
Decision rule: compare greedy gaps against baseline 6.30/5.39, 15.80/0.90, 26.99/16.62/10.94/5.91; keep if overall mean improves without broad regression.
